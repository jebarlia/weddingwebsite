Washington Dulles (IAD) About 35 minutes to Middleburg. Fly here if you can.
Reagan National (DCA) About an hour. More with Friday traffic.
Do we need a car?

Yes. This is horse country. Two-lane roads, stone walls, not many streetlights.

Ubers are rare out here, and rarer late at night. Plan your ride home before the night starts.

Goodstone has plenty of parking.

What should we wear?

Feel your best. Look your best.

Saturday It's a wedding, so dress up. Men, a sport coat and a tie. Women, a long dress. Past that, wear what makes you stand a little taller.
Friday Cocktail attire.
Sunday Whatever you slept in, almost.

October out here is warm at noon and cold by dark. Bring a coat you love. Wear shoes that can handle grass. Dress for the weather and you'll dance longer.

What is there to do?

If you come early, or need a few hours to kill:

Walk Middleburg One main street. Old stone buildings, saddle shops, bookstores, coffee. Takes an hour if you let it.
The National Sporting Library & Museum Horses, hounds and the paintings of both. Right in town.
Wineries Boxwood, Greenhill and Cana are all close. Sit outside and watch the hills change color.
Sky Meadows State Park About twenty minutes west. A real hike up to a real view of the Blue Ridge.
Aldie Mill A water mill from the early 1800s. Ten minutes east.
Downtown Leesburg Bigger, livelier, more bars. Good for Friday before the Red Fox.
