Which airport?
Washington Dulles (IAD) About 35 minutes to Middleburg. Fly here if you can.
Reagan National (DCA) About an hour. More with Friday traffic.
Do we need a car?

Yes. It's mostly back roads.

Ubers are hard to find, especially late. Figure out your ride home before the night starts.

There's plenty of parking at Goodstone.

What should we wear?

Feel your best, look your best.

Saturday It's a wedding, so dress up a bit. Guys, sport coat and tie. Ladies, long dress. Beyond that, wear whatever makes you feel good.
Friday Cocktail attire.
Sunday Anything goes.

October in Virginia is warm during the day and chilly at night. Bring a jacket, and wear shoes that'll work on grass.

What is there to do?

If you're in early or have a few hours to kill:

Walk Middleburg One main street with old stone buildings, good shops and coffee. Easy hour or two.
The National Sporting Library & Museum Horse and hunting art, right in town.
Wineries Boxwood, Greenhill and Cana are all close. Good way to spend an afternoon.
Sky Meadows State Park About 20 minutes west. Good hike, great view of the Blue Ridge.
Aldie Mill Old mill from the early 1800s, 10 minutes east.
Downtown Leesburg More restaurants and bars if you want a bigger town.
