Look your best, feel your best.

It's a wedding, so don't look like a schlep. But wear what makes you feel most confident for the occasion.

Friday — welcome drinks Cocktail attire. Guys: a nice button-down. No blazer necessary. Women: a cocktail dress, jumpsuit or dressy separates.
Saturday — the wedding Guys: a nice sport coat and tie. It doesn't have to be a suit, but that works too. Just feel your best. Women: a long dress. Part of the evening is on grass, so be prepared for that.
Sunday — brunch Anything goes. Come as you are.
Weather Early October in Middleburg is usually around 70° during the day and in the 40s at night. Bring a jacket or a wrap for after dark.
